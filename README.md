# Tottenham-transfer-impact-analysis

## Research Question

How do new player signings affect Tottenham Hotspur's team performance?

## Objective

This project analyses Tottenham Hotspur's performance before and after major player signings to understand whether new players are associated with measurable changes in team performance.

The analysis will consider both team-level and individual-player performance.

## Key Questions

- Does Tottenham's performance improve after major player signings?
- Which new signings have had the greatest impact?
- Does the impact differ by player position?
- Is transfer fee related to player performance?
- How quickly do new signings begin contributing?
- Does Tottenham perform differently when a new signing plays?
- What characteristics are associated with successful signings?

## Planned Analysis

1. Collect transfer and player-performance data
2. Clean and prepare the data
3. Analyse Tottenham's team performance before and after signings
4. Analyse individual player contribution
5. Compare transfer fees with player performance
6. Compare selected Tottenham seasons
7. Explore relationships between signings and team outcomes
8. Build visualizations to communicate findings

## Important Limitation

A change in team performance after a transfer does not necessarily mean that the transfer caused the change.

Other factors such as managerial changes, injuries, tactics, fixture difficulty and other transfers may also influence performance.

Therefore, this project will distinguish between correlation and causation wherever possible.
